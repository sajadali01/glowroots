Why Hair Fall Is So Common in Pakistan — And How Hair Growth Serum Can Help

📅 Published: June 2025  |  ✍️ By GlowRoots Hair Care Team  |  🏷️ Hair Growth, Hair Fall Treatment

Hair fall has become one of the most common concerns across Pakistan, affecting millions of people between the ages of 18 and 45. Whether you're in Karachi's humidity, Lahore's pollution, or Islamabad's changing seasons — your hair is constantly under attack.

What Causes Hair Fall in Pakistan?

The most frequent causes include hard water with high mineral content, nutritional deficiencies (especially biotin and iron), stress, heat damage from styling, and seasonal changes. Hormonal imbalances also play a major role.

How Does Hair Growth Serum Help?

A quality hair growth serum formulated with biotin, keratin, and natural oils targets hair fall at its root cause. Unlike shampoos that only clean the surface, a serum penetrates deep into the scalp to nourish follicles and stimulate circulation.

What to Look for in a Hair Growth Serum

Look for: (1) Biotin content for follicle strengthening, (2) Keratin for structural repair, (3) Natural oils for scalp nourishment, and (4) No harmful sulfates or parabens. GlowRoots contains all of these in one lightweight, non-greasy formula.

How Long Does It Take to See Results?

Most users notice reduced hair fall within 2 weeks of consistent daily use. Significant new growth typically appears within 30 days. Apply to a clean scalp every night before bed and massage gently for 2–3 minutes.
